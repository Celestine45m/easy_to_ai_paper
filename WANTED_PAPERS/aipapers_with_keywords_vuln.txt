[No.1] Impact of Dataset Properties on Membership Inference Vulnerability of Deep Transfer Learning
来源: NeurIPS 2025
命中: vuln
标题: 数据集特性对深度迁移学习成员推断漏洞的影响
类别: social and economic aspects of machine learning
作者: Marlon Tobaben;Hibiki Ito;Joonas Jälkö;Yuan He;Antti Honkela;
机构: CSC – IT Center for Science;University of Helsinki;Kyoto University;Aalto University;
类型: Poster
链接: https://openreview.net/forum?id=SR6ivhZCQK
arXi: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.06674
英摘: Membership inference attacks (MIAs) are used to test practical privacy of machine learning models. MIAs complement formal guarantees from differential privacy (DP) under a more realistic adversary model. We analyse MIA vulnerability of fine-tuned neural networks both empirically and theoretically, the latter using a simplified model of fine-tuning. We show that the vulnerability of non-DP models when measured as the attacker advantage at a fixed false positive rate reduces according to a simple power law as the number of examples per class increases. A similar power-law applies even for the most vulnerable points, but the dataset size needed for adequate protection of the most vulnerable points is very large.
中摘: 会员推断攻击（MIAs）用于测试机器学习模型的实际隐私。在更现实的对手模型下，MIAs补充了差分隐私（DP）提供的正式保障。我们从实证和理论两个方面分析了微调神经网络对MIA的脆弱性，后者使用了一个简化的微调模型。我们表明，当以固定误报率衡量攻击者的优势时，非DP模型的脆弱性会随着每个类别样本数量的增加而遵循简单的幂律减少。即使对于最脆弱的点，也适用类似的幂律，但要使最脆弱的点得到充分保护所需的数据集大小非常大。

[No.3] SAFEx: Analyzing Vulnerabilities of MoE-Based LLMs via Stable Safety-critical Expert Identification
来源: NeurIPS 2025
命中: vuln
标题: SAFEx：通过稳定的安全关键专家识别分析MoE架构的大语言模型的脆弱性
类别: social and economic aspects of machine learning
作者: ZhengLin Lai;Mengyao Liao;Bingzhe Wu;Dong Xu;Zebin Zhao;Zhihang Yuan;Chao Fan;Jianqiang Li;
机构: Shenzhen University;Tencent;;ByteDance;Infinigence AI;
类型: Poster
链接: https://openreview.net/forum?id=VwsXmcMyg5
arXi: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17368
英摘: Large language models with Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures achieve efficiency and scalability, yet their routing mechanisms introduce safety alignment challenges insufficiently addressed by techniques developed for dense models. In this work, the MoE-specific safety risk of positional vulnerability-that safety-aligned behaviors rely on specific expert modules-is formalized and systematically analyzed. An analytical framework, SAFEx, is presented to robustly identify, characterize, and validate safety-critical experts via a stability-based expert selection procedure, and to decompose them into two functional groups: the Harmful Content Detection Group (HCDG), which specializes in identifying and recognizing harmful content within user inputs, and the Harmful Response Control Group (HRCG), which specializes in controlling and enforcing model behaviors to generate appropriate safety responses. Expert-level interventions are conducted to probe causality and to test mitigation. Targeted masking of SAFEx-selected experts reveals that safety behavior is highly concentrated. On Qwen3-30B-A3B, configured with 48 MoE-FFN layers and 128 experts per layer under top-8 routing (48x128=6,144 experts in total), disabling 12 selected experts reduces the refusal rate by 22%. In addition, lightweight adaptation is performed using LoRA under three configurations-the HRCG, the union of HCDG and HRCG, and all experts-and the resulting updates are composed through negative weight merging targeted at the HRCG, leading to improved refusal under adversarial prompts without full-model retraining. These results establish positional vulnerability as a distinct MoE-specific safety challenge and provide a practical, compute-efficient pathway for expert-level safety interventions within routed architectures.
中摘: 大型语言模型采用专家混合（MoE）架构实现了效率和可扩展性，但其路由机制引入了安全对齐挑战，这些挑战没有被为密集型模型开发的技术充分解决。在本工作中，我们正式化并系统地分析了MoE特有的安全风险——位置脆弱性，即安全对齐的行为依赖于特定的专家模块。我们提出了一个分析框架SAFEx，通过基于稳定性的专家选择程序来稳健地识别、表征和验证关键安全专家，并将其分解为两个功能组：有害内容检测组（HCDG），专门识别和识别用户输入中的有害内容；以及有害响应控制组（HRCG），专门控制和强制模型行为以生成适当的安全响应。通过对选定专家进行干预，研究因果关系并测试缓解措施。针对SAFEx选定的专家进行的针对性屏蔽揭示了安全行为高度集中。在Qwen3-30B-A3B上，配置有48层MoE全连接前馈网络（FFN）层和每层128个专家，在top-8路由下（总共48x128=6,144个专家），禁用12个选定的专家可以减少拒绝率22%。此外，使用LoRA进行了轻量级适应，分别针对HRCG、HCDG与HRCG的联合以及所有专家进行，并通过针对HRCG的负权重合并组成结果更新，从而在不重新训练整个模型的情况下提高了对抗性提示下的拒绝率。这些结果确立了位置脆弱性作为MoE特有的独特安全挑战，并为路由架构内的专家级安全干预提供了一条实用且计算高效的路径。

[No.5] Unveiling and Mitigating Backdoor Vulnerabilities based on Unlearning Weight Changes and Backdoor Activeness
来源: NeurIPS 2024
命中: vuln
标题: 基于卸学权重变化和后门活跃度揭露与缓解后门漏洞
类别: safety in machine learning
作者: Weilin Lin;Li Liu;Shaokui Wei;Jianze Li;Hui Xiong;
机构: Hong Kong University of Science and Technology;Chinese University of Hong Kong;
类型: Poster
链接: https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/95499
arXi: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20291
英摘: The security threat of backdoor attacks is a central concern for deep neural networks (DNNs). Recently, without poisoned data, unlearning models with clean data and then learning a pruning mask have contributed to backdoor defense. Additionally, vanilla fine-tuning with those clean data can help recover the lost clean accuracy. However, the behavior of clean unlearning is still under-explored, and vanilla fine-tuning unintentionally induces back the backdoor effect. In this work, we first investigate model unlearning from the perspective of weight changes and gradient norms, and find two interesting observations in the backdoored model: 1) the weight changes between poison and clean unlearning are positively correlated, making it possible for us to identify the backdoored-related neurons without using poisoned data; 2) the neurons of the backdoored model are more active (i.e., larger changes in gradient norm) than those in the clean model, suggesting the need to suppress the gradient norm during fine-tuning. Then, we propose an effective two-stage defense method. In the first stage, an efficient Neuron Weight Change (NWC)-based Backdoor Reinitialization is proposed based on observation 1). In the second stage, based on observation 2), we design an Activeness-Aware Fine-Tuning to replace the vanilla fine-tuning. Extensive experiments, involving eight backdoor attacks on three benchmark datasets, demonstrate the superior performance of our proposed method compared to recent state-of-the-art backdoor defense approaches.
中摘: 后门攻击的安全威胁是深度神经网络（DNN）的核心关注点。最近，使用干净数据取消学习模型，然后学习一个剪枝掩码，即使没有被污染的数据也对防御后门做出了贡献。此外，用这些干净数据进行纯调整可以帮助恢复失去的干净准确度。然而，干净取消学习的行为仍未得到充分探索，而纯调整无意中重新引入了后门效应。在本工作中，我们首先从权重变化和梯度范数的角度研究模型取消学习，并在被植入后门的模型中发现了两个有趣的现象：1）毒化和干净取消学习之间的权重变化呈正相关，这使我们能够在不使用毒化数据的情况下识别与后门相关的神经元；2）被植入后门的模型的神经元比干净模型中的神经元更活跃（即，梯度范数的变化更大），表明在微调过程中需要抑制梯度范数。然后，我们提出了一种有效的两阶段防御方法。第一阶段，基于观察1，我们提出了高效的基于神经元权重变化（NWC）的后门重初始化。第二阶段，根据观察2，我们设计了一种活性感知微调来取代纯调整。广泛的实验，涵盖了三种基准数据集上的八种后门攻击，证明了我们提出的方法相比近期最先进的后门防御方法具有优越的性能。

[No.6] On provable privacy vulnerabilities of graph representations
来源: NeurIPS 2024
命中: vuln
标题: 可证明的图表示中的隐私漏洞
类别: privacy
作者: Ruofan Wu;Guanhua Fang;Mingyang Zhang;Qiying Pan;Tengfei LIU;Weiqiang Wang;
机构: Ant Group;Fudan University;Shanghai Jiao Tong University;
类型: Poster
链接: https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/95590
arXi: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04033
英摘: Graph representation learning (GRL) is critical for extracting insights from complex network structures, but it also raises security concerns due to potential privacy vulnerabilities in these representations. This paper investigates the structural vulnerabilities in graph neural models where sensitive topological information can be inferred through edge reconstruction attacks. Our research primarily addresses the theoretical underpinnings of similarity-based edge reconstruction attacks (SERA), furnishing a non-asymptotic analysis of their reconstruction capacities. Moreover, we present empirical corroboration indicating that such attacks can perfectly reconstruct sparse graphs as graph size increases. Conversely, we establish that sparsity is a critical factor for SERA's effectiveness, as demonstrated through analysis and experiments on (dense) stochastic block models. Finally, we explore the resilience of private graph representations produced via noisy aggregation (NAG) mechanism against SERA. Through theoretical analysis and empirical assessments, we affirm the mitigation of SERA using NAG . In parallel, we also empirically delineate instances wherein SERA demonstrates both efficacy and deficiency in its capacity to function as an instrument for elucidating the trade-off between privacy and utility.
中摘: 图表示学习（GRL）对于从复杂网络结构中提取洞见至关重要，但同时也因这些表示中的潜在隐私漏洞而引发安全问题。本文探讨了图神经模型中的结构脆弱性，其中敏感的拓扑信息可能通过边重构攻击被推断出来。我们的研究主要关注基于相似性的边重构攻击（SERA）的理论基础，提供了其重构能力的非渐近分析。此外，我们提出了实证证据，表明随着图大小的增加，此类攻击可以完美地重构稀疏图。相反，我们证明了稀疏性是SERA有效性的一个关键因素，这在对（密集）随机块模型的分析和实验中得到了展示。最后，我们研究了通过噪声聚合（NAG）机制生成的私有图表示对SERA的抗性。通过理论分析和实证评估，我们证实了使用NAG可以缓解SERA的影响。同时，我们也实证地描绘了一些场景，在这些场景中，SERA在其作为揭示隐私与效用之间权衡的工具的能力上既有效又有限。

[No.7] Reconstruction Attacks on Machine Unlearning: Simple Models are Vulnerable
来源: NeurIPS 2024
命中: vuln
标题: 机器遗忘中的重建攻击：简单模型易受攻击
类别: privacy
作者: Martin Andres Bertran;Shuai Tang;Michael Kearns;Jamie Heather Morgenstern;Aaron Roth;Steven Wu;
机构: Amazon;University of Pennsylvania;Carnegie Mellon University;
类型: Poster
链接: https://neurips.cc/virtual/2024/poster/94032
arXi: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20272
英摘: Machine unlearning is motivated by desire for data autonomy: a person can request to have their data's influence removed from deployed models, and those models should be updated as if they were retrained without the person's data. We show that, counter-intuitively, these updates expose individuals to high-accuracy reconstruction attacks which allow the attacker to recover their data in its entirety, even when the original models are so simple that privacy risk might not otherwise have been a concern. We show how to mount a near-perfect attack on the deleted data point from linear regression models. We then generalize our attack to other loss functions and architectures, and empirically demonstrate the effectiveness of our attacks across a wide range of datasets (capturing both tabular and image data). Our work highlights that privacy risk is significant even for extremely simple model classes when individuals can request deletion of their data from the model.
中摘: 机器遗忘的概念源于对数据自主权的渴望：个人可以请求移除其数据对已部署模型的影响，而这些模型应进行更新，就像在没有该个人数据的情况下重新训练一样。我们展示了一个反直觉的现象：这些更新使个体面临高精度重建攻击的风险，这种攻击允许攻击者几乎完整地恢复他们的数据，即使原始模型如此简单，以至于隐私风险可能原本不值得担忧。我们展示了如何从线性回归模型中对被删除的数据点发起近乎完美的攻击。然后，我们将攻击泛化到其他损失函数和架构，并在广泛的数据集（包括表格和图像数据）上实证展示了我们攻击的有效性。我们的工作强调了，当个人可以从模型中请求删除他们的数据时，即使是极其简单的模型类别，隐私风险也是显著的。

类别: 
作者: Aoxiang Zhang;Yu Ran;Weixuan Tang;Yuan-Gen Wang;

英摘: No-Reference Video Quality Assessment (NR-VQA) plays an essential role in improving the viewing experience of end-users. Driven by deep learning, recent NR-VQA models based on Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and Transformers have achieved outstanding performance. To build a reliable and practical assessment system, it is of great necessity to evaluate their robustness. However, such issue has received little attention in the academic community. In this paper, we make the first attempt to evaluate the robustness of NR-VQA models against adversarial attacks, and propose a patch-based random search method for black-box attack. Specifically, considering both the attack effect on quality score and the visual quality of adversarial video, the attack problem is formulated as misleading the estimated quality score under the constraint of just-noticeable difference (JND). Built upon such formulation, a novel loss function called Score-Reversed Boundary Loss is designed to push the adversarial video's estimated quality score far away from its ground-truth score towards a specific boundary, and the JND constraint is modeled as a strict $L_2$ and $L_\infty$ norm restriction. By this means, both white-box and black-box attacks can be launched in an effective and imperceptible manner. The source code is available at https://github.com/GZHU-DVL/AttackVQA.

[No.9] BadJudge: Backdoor Vulnerabilities of LLM-As-A-Judge
来源: ICLR 2025
命中: vuln
标题: BadJudge: LLM-as-a-Judge的后门漏洞
类别: alignment, fairness, safety, privacy, and societal considerations
作者: Terry Tong;Fei Wang;Zhe Zhao;Muhao Chen;
机构: University of California, Davis;University of Southern California;
类型: Poster
链接: https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/28942
arXi: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.00596
英摘: This paper proposes a novel backdoor threat attacking the LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation regime, where the adversary controls both the candidate and evaluator model. The backdoored evaluator victimizes benign users by unfairly assigning inflated scores to adversary. A trivial single token backdoor poisoning 1% of the evaluator training data triples the adversary's score with respect to their legitimate score. We systematically categorize levels of data access corresponding to three real-world settings, (1) web poisoning, (2) malicious annotator, and (3) weight poisoning. These regimes reflect a weak to strong escalation of data access that highly correlates with attack severity. Under the weakest assumptions - web poisoning (1), the adversary still induces a 20% score inflation. Likewise, in the (3) weight poisoning regime, the stronger assumptions enable the adversary to inflate their scores from 1.5/5 to 4.9/5. The backdoor threat generalizes across different evaluator architectures, trigger designs, evaluation tasks, and poisoning rates. By poisoning 10% of the evaluator training data, we control toxicity judges (Guardrails) to misclassify toxic prompts as non-toxic 89% of the time, and document reranker judges in RAG to rank the poisoned document first 97% of the time. LLM-as-a-Judge is uniquely positioned at the intersection of ethics and technology, where social implications of mislead model selection and evaluation constrain the available defensive tools. Amidst these challenges, model merging emerges as a principled tool to offset the backdoor, reducing ASR to near 0% whilst maintaining SOTA performance. Model merging's low computational cost and convenient integration into the current LLM Judge training pipeline position it as a promising avenue for backdoor mitigation in the LLM-as-a-Judge setting.
中摘: 本文提出了一种针对LLM作为裁判的评估体系的新颖后门威胁攻击，其中攻击者同时控制候选模型和评估模型。被植入后门的评估者通过不公平地给予攻击者过高的评分来伤害无辜用户。即使在评估者训练数据中仅用1%的比例进行简单的单令牌后门投毒，也能使攻击者的得分相对于其合法得分翻三倍。我们系统地将数据访问级别分类为三种现实世界场景：(1)网络投毒，(2)恶意标注员，以及(3)权重投毒。这些体系反映了从弱到强的数据访问升级，与攻击的严重程度高度相关。在最弱假设下——即网络投毒(1)，攻击者仍能诱导出20%的分数膨胀。同样，在(3)权重投毒体系中，更强的假设让攻击者能够将其分数从1.5/5提升至4.9/5。这种后门威胁在不同的评估者架构、触发器设计、评估任务和投毒率上都具有泛化性。通过对评估者训练数据的10%进行投毒，我们能够操控毒性裁判（Guardrails），使其将有害提示错误分类为无害提示89%的时间，以及在RAG中的文档重新排序裁判，使其97%的时间将被投毒的文档排在首位。LLM作为裁判的独特定位在于伦理和技术的交汇处，误导模型选择和评估的社会影响限制了可用的防御工具。面对这些挑战，模型融合作为一种原则性的工具出现，可以抵消后门的影响，将ASR降低到接近0%，同时保持SOTA（State-Of-The-Art，最先进的）性能。模型融合的低计算成本和方便集成到当前LLM裁判训练管道的位置，使其成为在LLM作为裁判设置中缓解后门问题的有希望途径。

[No.10] Rethinking Audio-Visual Adversarial Vulnerability from Temporal and Modality Perspectives
来源: ICLR 2025
命中: vuln
标题: 从时间和模态的角度重新思考音视频对抗性漏洞
类别: alignment, fairness, safety, privacy, and societal considerations
作者: Zeliang Zhang;Susan Liang;Daiki Shimada;Chenliang Xu;
机构: University of Rochester;Sony Group Corporation;
类型: Poster
链接: https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/28927
arXi: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11858
英摘: While audio-visual learning equips models with a richer understanding of the real world by leveraging multiple sensory modalities, this integration also introduces new vulnerabilities to adversarial attacks.
  In this paper, we present a comprehensive study of the adversarial robustness of audio-visual models, considering both temporal and modality-specific vulnerabilities. We propose two powerful adversarial attacks: 1) a temporal invariance attack that exploits the inherent temporal redundancy across consecutive time segments and 2) a modality misalignment attack that introduces incongruence between the audio and visual modalities. These attacks are designed to thoroughly assess the robustness of audio-visual models against diverse threats. Furthermore, to defend against such attacks, we introduce a novel audio-visual adversarial training framework. This framework addresses key challenges in vanilla adversarial training by incorporating efficient adversarial perturbation crafting tailored to multi-modal data and an adversarial curriculum strategy.
