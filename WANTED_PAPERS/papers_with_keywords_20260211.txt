[No.1] LOKI: A Comprehensive Synthetic Data Detection Benchmark using Large Multimodal Models
来源: ICLR 2025
命中: detection
标题: LOKI：使用大型多模态模型的综合合成数据检测基准
类别: datasets and benchmarks
作者: Junyan Ye;Baichuan Zhou;Zilong Huang;Junan Zhang;Tianyi Bai;Hengrui Kang;Jun He;Honglin Lin;Zihao Wang;Tong Wu;Zhizheng Wu;Yiping Chen;Dahua Lin;Conghui He;Weijia Li;
机构: Sun Yat-sen University;Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory;Chinese University of Hong Kong;Hong Kong University of Science and Technology;Shanghai Jiao Tong University;;Stanford University;
类型: Spotlight
链接: https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/27689
arXi: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.09732
英摘: With the rapid development of AI-generated content, the future internet may be inundated with synthetic data, making the discrimination of authentic and credible multimodal data increasingly challenging. Synthetic data detection has thus garnered widespread attention, and the performance of large multimodal models (LMMs) in this task has attracted significant interest. LMMs can provide natural language explanations for their authenticity judgments, enhancing the explainability of synthetic content detection. Simultaneously, the task of distinguishing between real and synthetic data effectively tests the perception, knowledge, and reasoning capabilities of LMMs. In response, we introduce LOKI, a novel benchmark designed to evaluate the ability of LMMs to detect synthetic data across multiple modalities. LOKI encompasses video, image, 3D, text, and audio modalities, comprising 18K carefully curated questions across 26 subcategories with clear difficulty levels. The benchmark includes coarse-grained judgment and multiple-choice questions, as well as fine-grained anomaly selection and explanation tasks, allowing for a comprehensive analysis of LMMs. We evaluated 22 open-source LMMs and 6 closed-source models on LOKI, highlighting their potential as synthetic data detectors and also revealing some limitations in the development of LMM capabilities. More information about LOKI can be found at https://opendatalab.github.io/LOKI/
中摘: 随着AI生成内容的快速发展，未来的互联网可能会被合成数据淹没，使得区分真实可靠的多模态数据越来越具有挑战性。因此，合成数据检测引起了广泛的关注，大型多模态模型（LMMs）在这项任务中的表现也吸引了大量兴趣。LMMs可以为它们的真实性和判断提供自然语言解释，增强合成内容检测的可解释性。同时，区分真实和合成数据的任务有效地测试了LMMs的感知、知识和推理能力。为此，我们引入了LOKI，这是一种新型基准，用于评估LMMs在多种模态下检测合成数据的能力。LOKI包含视频、图像、3D、文本和音频等多种模态，涵盖了26个子类别中的18000个精心策划的问题，并且这些问题具有明确的难度级别。该基准包括粗粒度判断和多项选择题，以及细粒度异常选择和解释任务，允许对LMMs进行全面分析。我们在LOKI上评估了22个开源LMMs和6个闭源模型，突显了它们作为合成数据检测器的潜力，同时也揭示了一些LMM能力发展中存在的局限性。更多关于LOKI的信息可以在https://opendatalab.github.io/LOKI/找到。

[No.2] MOS: Model Synergy for Test-Time Adaptation on LiDAR-Based 3D Object Detection
来源: ICLR 2025
命中: detection
标题: MOS：基于LiDAR的3D物体检测测试时适应性模型协同
类别: transfer learning, meta learning, and lifelong learning
作者: Zhuoxiao Chen;Junjie Meng;Mahsa Baktashmotlagh;Yonggang Zhang;Zi Huang;Yadan Luo;
机构: University of Queensland;University of Electronic Science and Technology of China;Hong Kong Baptist University;
类型: Oral
链接: https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/29272
arXi: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14878
英摘: LiDAR-based 3D object detection is crucial for various applications but often experiences performance degradation in real-world deployments due to domain shifts. While most studies focus on cross-dataset shifts, such as changes in environments and object geometries, practical corruptions from sensor variations and weather conditions remain underexplored. In this work, we propose a novel online test-time adaptation framework for 3D detectors that effectively tackles these shifts, including a challenging cross-corruption scenario where cross-dataset shifts and corruptions co-occur. By leveraging long-term knowledge from previous test batches, our approach mitigates catastrophic forgetting and adapts effectively to diverse shifts. Specifically, we propose a Model Synergy (MOS) strategy that dynamically selects historical checkpoints with diverse knowledge and assembles them to best accommodate the current test batch. This assembly is directed by our proposed Synergy Weights (SW), which perform a weighted averaging of the selected checkpoints, minimizing redundancy in the composite model. The SWs are computed by evaluating the similarity of predicted bounding boxes on the test data and the independence of features between checkpoint pairs in the model bank. To maintain an efficient and informative model bank, we discard checkpoints with the lowest average SW scores, replacing them with newly updated models. Our method was rigorously tested against existing test-time adaptation strategies across three datasets and eight types of corruptions, demonstrating superior adaptability to dynamic scenes and conditions. Notably, it achieved a 67.3% improvement in a challenging cross-corruption scenario, offering a more comprehensive benchmark for adaptation. Source code: https://github.com/zhuoxiao-chen/MOS.
中摘: 基于LiDAR的3D物体检测在各种应用中至关重要，但在实际部署中常常由于领域偏移而性能下降。虽然大多数研究集中在跨数据集的偏移上，如环境和物体几何形状的变化，但来自传感器变异和天气条件的实际干扰却很少被探索。在这项工作中，我们提出了一种新颖的在线测试时自适应框架，用于有效解决这些偏移问题，包括一个具有挑战性的跨干扰场景，其中跨数据集偏移和干扰同时发生。通过利用之前测试批次中的长期知识，我们的方法减轻了灾难性遗忘，并能够有效地适应各种偏移。具体而言，我们提出了一个模型协同（MOS）策略，该策略动态选择具有多样化知识的历史检查点，并将它们组装起来以最好地适应当前的测试批次。这一组装过程由我们提出的协同权重（SW）指导，协同权重对选定的检查点进行加权平均，从而最小化复合模型中的冗余。SWs通过评估测试数据上预测边界框的相似性和模型银行中检查点对之间的特征独立性来计算。为了保持高效且信息丰富的模型库，我们丢弃具有最低平均SW分数的检查点，并用新更新的模型替换它们。我们的方法在三个数据集和八种类型的干扰下与现有的测试时自适应策略进行了严格对比，展示了在动态场景和条件下更出色的自适应能力。值得注意的是，在一个具有挑战性的跨干扰场景中，它实现了67.3%的性能提升，为自适应提供了一个更为全面的基准。源代码：https://github.com/zhuoxiao-chen/MOS。

[No.4] GOLD: Graph Out-of-Distribution Detection via Implicit Adversarial Latent Generation
来源: ICLR 2025
命中: detection
标题: GOLD：基于隐式对抗潜在生成的图域外检测
类别: learning on graphs and other geometries & topologies
作者: Danny Wang;Ruihong Qiu;Guangdong Bai;Zi Huang;
机构: University of Queensland;;
类型: Spotlight
链接: https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/27751
arXi: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05780
英摘: Despite graph neural networks' (GNNs) great success in modelling graph-structured data, out-of-distribution (OOD) test instances still pose a great challenge for current GNNs. One of the most effective techniques to detect OOD nodes is to expose the detector model with an additional OOD node-set, yet the extra OOD instances are often difficult to obtain in practice. Recent methods for image data address this problem using OOD data synthesis, typically relying on pre-trained generative models like Stable Diffusion. However, these approaches require vast amounts of additional data, as well as one-for-all pre-trained generative models, which are not available for graph data. Therefore, we propose the GOLD framework for graph OOD detection, an implicit adversarial learning pipeline with synthetic OOD exposure without pre-trained models. The implicit adversarial training process employs a novel alternating optimisation framework by training: (1) a latent generative model to regularly imitate the in-distribution (ID) embeddings from an evolving GNN, and (2) a GNN encoder and an OOD detector to accurately classify ID data while increasing the energy divergence between the ID embeddings and the generative model's synthetic embeddings. This novel approach implicitly transforms the synthetic embeddings into pseudo-OOD instances relative to the ID data, effectively simulating exposure to OOD scenarios without auxiliary data. Extensive OOD detection experiments are conducted on five benchmark graph datasets, verifying the superior performance of GOLD without using real OOD data compared with the state-of-the-art OOD exposure and non-exposure baselines.
中摘: 尽管图神经网络（GNNs）在建模图结构数据方面取得了巨大成功，但当前的GNNs仍然难以应对离分布（OOD）测试实例。检测OOD节点最有效的方法之一是通过向检测器模型暴露额外的OOD节点集，然而这些额外的OOD实例在实践中往往难以获得。针对图像数据，最近的方法使用OOD数据合成来解决这个问题，通常依赖于预训练生成模型，如Stable Diffusion。然而，这些方法需要大量的额外数据，并且依赖于通用的预训练生成模型，而这些对于图数据来说并不适用。因此，我们提出了GOLD框架，这是一种无需预训练模型的隐式对抗学习管道，用于图结构数据的OOD检测。该隐式对抗训练过程采用了一种新颖的交替优化框架，通过训练：（1）一个潜在生成模型定期模仿不断进化的GNN中的ID嵌入；（2）一个GNN编码器和一个OOD检测器准确分类ID数据，同时增加ID嵌入与生成模型合成嵌入之间的能量差异。这一新颖的方法隐式地将合成嵌入转换为相对于ID数据的伪OOD实例，从而有效地模拟了对OOD场景的暴露，而无需辅助数据。我们在五个基准图数据集上进行了广泛的OOD检测实验，验证了GOLD在不使用真实OOD数据的情况下，相较于最先进的OOD暴露和非暴露基线，具有更优的性能。

[No.5] The Power of LLM-Generated Synthetic Data for Stance Detection in Online Political Discussions
来源: ICLR 2025
命中: detection
标题: LLM生成的合成数据在在线政治讨论立场检测中的力量
类别: other topics in machine learning (i.e., none of the above)
作者: Stefan Sylvius Wagner;Maike Behrendt;Marc Ziegele;Stefan Harmeling;
机构: Heinrich-Heine-Universität Düsseldorf;Heinrich-Heine Universität;Technische Universität Dortmund;

arXi: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12480
英摘: Stance detection holds great potential to improve online political discussions through its deployment in discussion platforms for purposes such as content moderation, topic summarization or to facilitate more balanced discussions. Typically, transformer-based models are employed directly for stance detection, requiring vast amounts of data. However, the wide variety of debate topics in online political discussions makes data collection particularly challenging. LLMs have revived stance detection, but their online deployment in online political discussions faces challenges like inconsistent outputs, biases, and vulnerability to adversarial attacks. We show how LLM-generated synthetic data can improve stance detection for online political discussions by using reliable traditional stance detection models for online deployment, while leveraging the text generation capabilities of LLMs for synthetic data generation in a secure offline environment. To achieve this, (i) we generate synthetic data for specific debate questions by prompting a Mistral-7B model and show that fine-tuning with the generated synthetic data can substantially improve the performance of stance detection, while remaining interpretable and aligned with real world data. (ii) Using the synthetic data as a reference, we can improve performance even further by identifying the most informative samples in an unlabelled dataset, i.e., those samples which the stance detection model is most uncertain about and can benefit from the most. By fine-tuning with both synthetic data and the most informative samples, we surpass the performance of the baseline model that is fine-tuned on all true labels, while labelling considerably less data.
中摘: 立场检测在通过讨论平台的应用（如内容审核、主题总结或促进更平衡的讨论）来改善在线政治对话方面具有巨大潜力。通常，会直接使用基于变换器的模型进行立场检测，这需要大量的数据。然而，在线政治讨论中广泛多样的辩论话题使得数据收集尤其具有挑战性。大语言模型（LLMs）重新焕发了立场检测的应用，但在在线政治讨论中的部署却面临不一致输出、偏见和对抗攻击漏洞等挑战。我们展示了如何通过利用可靠的传统立场检测模型进行在线部署，并借助大语言模型的文本生成能力在安全的离线环境中生成合成数据，从而改进在线政治讨论中的立场检测。具体而言，(i) 我们通过提示一个 Mistral-7B 模型生成特定辩论问题的合成数据，并证明使用生成的合成数据进行微调可以显著提高立场检测的性能，同时保持可解释性和与真实世界数据的一致性。(ii) 利用合成数据作为参考，我们可以进一步提高性能，通过识别未标记数据集中最具有信息量的样本，即那些立场检测模型最不确定且最能从中受益的样本。通过结合使用合成数据和最具有信息量的样本进行微调，我们的模型超越了仅在所有真实标签上进行微调的基本模型，同时显著减少了需要标注的数据量。

[No.6] Union-over-Intersections: Object Detection beyond Winner-Takes-All
来源: ICLR 2025
命中: detection
标题: 并集超越交集：超越赢家通吃的人工智能对象检测
类别: applications to computer vision, audio, language, and other modalities
作者: Aritra Bhowmik;Pascal Mettes;Martin R. Oswald;Cees G. M. Snoek;
机构: University of Amsterdam;
类型: Spotlight
链接: https://iclr.cc/virtual/2025/poster/30208
arXi: https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18512
英摘: This paper revisits the problem of predicting box locations in object detection architectures. Typically, each box proposal or box query aims to directly maximize the intersection-over-union score with the ground truth, followed by a winner-takes-all non-maximum suppression where only the highest scoring box in each region is retained. We observe that both steps are sub-optimal: the first involves regressing proposals to the entire ground truth, which is a difficult task even with large receptive fields, and the second neglects valuable information from boxes other than the top candidate. Instead of regressing proposals to the whole ground truth, we propose a simpler approach: regress only to the area of intersection between the proposal and the ground truth. This avoids the need for proposals to extrapolate beyond their visual scope, improving localization accuracy. Rather than adopting a winner-takes-all strategy, we take the union over the regressed intersections of all boxes in a region to generate the final box outputs. Our plug-and-play method integrates seamlessly into proposal-based, grid-based, and query-based detection architectures with minimal modifications, consistently improving object localization and instance segmentation. We demonstrate its broad applicability and versatility across various detection and segmentation tasks.
中摘: 本文重新审视了物体检测架构中预测边界框位置的问题。通常，每个边界框提案或查询的目标是直接最大化其与真实框的交并比（IoU）分数，随后通过一个“胜者全拿”的非极大值抑制策略，仅保留每个区域中得分最高的边界框。我们观察到这两个步骤都是次优的：第一步涉及到将提案回归到整个真实框，即使具有大的感受野，这也是一项困难的任务；第二步忽略了除最高候选框外其他框中的宝贵信息。我们不将提案回归到整个真实框，而是提出一种更简单的办法：仅将提案回归到其与真实框相交的区域。这避免了提案需要超出其视觉范围进行外推的需求，从而提高了定位精度。我们不再采用“胜者全拿”的策略，而是将一个区域中所有框的回归交集取并集，生成最终的边界框输出。我们的即插即用方法可以无缝集成到基于提案、基于网格和基于查询的检测架构中，并且只需要少量修改，就能一致地提高物体定位和实例分割的准确性。我们展示了该方法在各种检测和分割任务中的广泛适用性和灵活性。

英摘: Single point supervised oriented object detection has gained attention and made initial progress within the community. Diverse from those approaches relying on one-shot samples or powerful pretrained models (e.g. SAM), PointOBB has shown promise due to its prior-free feature. In this paper, we propose PointOBB-v2, a simpler, faster, and stronger method to generate pseudo rotated boxes from points without relying on any other prior. Specifically, we first generate a Class Probability Map (CPM) by training the network with non-uniform positive and negative sampling. We show that the CPM is able to learn the approximate object regions and their contours. Then, Principal Component Analysis (PCA) is applied to accurately estimate the orientation and the boundary of objects. By further incorporating a separation mechanism, we resolve the confusion caused by the overlapping on the CPM, enabling its operation in high-density scenarios. Extensive comparisons demonstrate that our method achieves a training speed 15.58x faster and an accuracy improvement of 11.60%/25.15%/21.19% on the DOTA-v1.0/v1.5/v2.0 datasets compared to the previous state-of-the-art, PointOBB. This significantly advances the cutting edge of single point supervised oriented detection in the modular track.

英摘: Watermarking has emerged as a promising way to detect LLM-generated text, by augmenting LLM generations with later detectable signals. Recent work has proposed multiple families of watermarking schemes, several of which focus on preserving the LLM distribution. This distribution-preservation property is motivated by the fact that it is a tractable proxy for retaining LLM capabilities, as well as the inherently implied undetectability of the watermark by downstream users. Yet, despite much discourse around undetectability, no prior work has investigated the practical detectability of any of the current watermarking schemes in a realistic black-box setting. In this work we tackle this for the first time, developing rigorous statistical tests to detect the presence, and estimate parameters, of all three popular watermarking scheme families, using only a limited number of black-box queries. 英摘: Recent advances in AI-generated voices have intensified the challenge of detecting deepfake audio, posing risks for scams and the spread of disinformation. Last, we filter out poisoned samples within the identified target class(es) based on the clear separation between poisoned and clean samples. Extensive experiments under various settings of backdoor attacks demonstrate the superior detection performance of the proposed method to existing poisoned detection approaches according to sample activation-based metrics.
中摘: 这项工作研究了中毒样本检测的任务，以防御基于后门的数据中毒攻击。其核心挑战在于找到一个可泛化且区分性强的度量标准，以区分干净样本和各种类型的中毒样本（例如不同类型的触发器、不同的中毒比例）。受后门攻击中常见现象的启发，即被植入后门的模型倾向于在目标类内将显著不同的干净样本和中毒样本映射到相似的激活区域，我们引入了一种新的视角，称为样本激活梯度的圆分布（Gradient Circular Distribution，GCD）。基于GCD，我们发现了两个有趣的现象：一是目标类中的样本圆分布比干净类中的样本圆分布更为分散；二是目标类的GCD中，中毒样本和干净样本明显分离。受到这两个观察的启发，我们开发了一种创新的三阶段中毒样本检测方法，称为基于激活梯度的中毒样本检测（Activation Gradient based Poisoned sample Detection，AGPD）。首先，我们计算可信数据集训练模型后所有类别的GCD。然后，我们根据目标类与干净类之间的圆分布分散度差异来识别目标类。最后，我们根据中毒样本和干净样本之间的明显分离来过滤出目标类中的中毒样本。在各种后门攻击设置下的广泛实验表明，根据样本激活度量标准，所提出的方法在中毒样本检测性能上优于现有的检测方法。
